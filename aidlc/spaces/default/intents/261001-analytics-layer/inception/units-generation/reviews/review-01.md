### Summary

The structural contract is clean — the edge block parses, is acyclic, kinds are valid, the skeleton resolves to U1, and all 36 story ids are assigned with no GAP. The problem is semantic: the unit DAG is derived from U1's *summary slice* rather than from U1's *full scope*, so the dependency U1's terms handler actually needs on U2 is omitted, a weaker U3→U2 edge is present instead, and `US6.2` is credited to U1 while part of its acceptance criteria is delivered by U3. Three Major findings, so NOT-READY; the human should weigh whether the skeleton rule is being satisfied by omitting a real edge.

**Could not verify:** the "frozen two-operation interface" the design cites (dependency.md, "U2 exposes a frozen two-operation interface that U1 consumes") has no artifact yet — `contract-design` has not run for this intent, so the freeze is asserted, not recorded; and the dependency doc's illustrative orderings (`u1 → u2 → u3 → u4`, …) are borderline against the topology-only rule, though explicitly framed as demonstrating that multiple orders exist rather than recommending one.
