### Summary

The decomposition is sound and, unusually, faithfully grounded in the real code (`routes.py` reaching `repository` directly, the private `_WORD` regex in `dummy_client.py:68`, the feature-scoped two-new-components claim); the catalogue is well-formed and the traceability is honest, so the artifact reaches READY. The one Major issue is a decision-record contradiction: ADR-009 asserts the platform stories are marked `GAP` while the file itself uses the sensor-supported `N/A` — worth correcting before a later reader acts on the ADR and trips the traceability gate. The remaining items are precision defects (entity identifier/attribute consistency, a miscount in the consolidated summary, two under-documented dependency couplings) that a downstream stage should not be misled by but that do not block.
