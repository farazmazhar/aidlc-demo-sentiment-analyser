### Summary

The intent analysis, the discipline of refusal (no fabricated zero, no silent clamp, 200-never-404), the boundary-denial paragraph in NFR4, and the refusal to resurrect `intensity` are all strong and correctly traced. What blocks this is a set of unresolved boundary cases on the two new endpoints and one self-contradiction: FR8.4 mandates a reproducing test for R-01 that no requirement fixes while FR8.5 mandates a green suite, and A5/NFR5 claim the sentiment adapters are untouched while FR4.5 mandates editing one of them. Every FR in FR2 and FR3 lacks a ruling for at least one of the boundary inputs the feature will actually receive (inverted range, `import_id` matching nothing, extreme `limit`), the share unit (A4) is an invented wire decision left open, FR8.2 demands pinned expected values the artifact does not contain, and NFR1's "no unbounded result set exists" is false as written. Coverage of the four stated capabilities and the eight enumerated practices obligations is otherwise complete, and the `import_id` extension to the terms endpoint is consistent with the description — Q9 asked, the human chose it, and the consolidated summary confirmed it — but the upstream `scope-document.md` and `intent-statement.md` success metric still read without it, so the divergence is not recorded as an amendment.
