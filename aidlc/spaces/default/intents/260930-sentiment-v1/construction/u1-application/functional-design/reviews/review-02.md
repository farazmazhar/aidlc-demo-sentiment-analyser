### Summary

The design is internally well structured but fails its own acceptance contract in two places a developer cannot resolve without guessing: the engine-resolution model cannot express the "live requested, no key" state BR1.4 refuses on (R-01), and the artifacts contradict the pinned `/v1` contract, both in the error envelope's `details` member (R-03) and in returning a stored legacy `intensity` under an `additionalProperties: false` schema (R-04). Two assigned criteria are also marked covered although their rules supply neither the default limit nor the refusal status/code the criteria require (R-02), and the stage's `upstream-coverage` sensor fails outright (R-05); the remaining findings are minor cross-reference and rule-coverage gaps.
